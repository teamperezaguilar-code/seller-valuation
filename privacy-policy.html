Team Perez Aguilar, operating under San Fernando Realty ("we," "us," or "our"), respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through our website tools, how we use it, and the choices available to you, particularly if you are a California resident.

Information We Collect

When you use our home valuation tool or request a consultation, we collect the information you provide directly, which may include:

  • Your name
  • Property address
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Preferred contact date and time
  • Any additional message you choose to share

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Generate a preliminary home value estimate
  • Respond to your consultation request
  • Contact you by phone, text, or email about your inquiry
  • Send you relevant market information, if you have not opted out

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Third-Party Services We Use

To operate this website, we rely on the following third-party services, each of which processes limited data on our behalf:

  • RentCast — processes the property address you submit to generate an automated home value estimate.
  • Formspree — processes consultation request form submissions and delivers them to our team by email.

These providers may retain data according to their own privacy policies, which are separate from ours.

Your California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we have collected about you
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information (we do not sell personal information)
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information below.

SMS and Email Communications

By submitting a form on this site, you may be contacted by phone, text message, or email regarding your request. Message and data rates may apply to text messages. You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP, and out of email by using the unsubscribe link in any message.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
